## Venues

Venues now have their own pages. Theaters, warehouses, escape room buildings and other places that host immersive work each get a page with their address, a map and the shows playing there. When you list an event, you can pick its venue so the two are connected.

If you run a venue, you can claim its page from the venue page. Once it is yours, it shows up under **Venues** in your menu and you can keep it up to date.

![The PHI Centre venue page in Montreal, with its map and the shows that played there](/images/changelog/venue-page.jpg)

## Show runs

Shows that tour to a new city, come back each year or remount are now grouped into a show run. Each city or season is still its own listing, but they all live under one show, so it is easy to see every run in one place.

![The Faulty Towers show run, with every city listed under one show](/images/changelog/show-run.jpg)

When you copy an event for a new city, the copy asks to join the same show run, and our team confirms it when they review the event.

## Search by country or state

You can now search a whole country or state, like **France**, **Germany** or **California**, and see events across all of it. City searches work the same as before.

![A search for France showing events across the whole country on the map](/images/changelog/country-search.jpg)

## Go-live time

When you schedule an event to go live later, you can now choose the time as well as the day. The time is in the event's own time zone.

We also made the picker easier to use on a phone. The time now sits right above the buttons instead of below every month, and the page behind it no longer scrolls while it is open.

![The go-live picker with a day and a time chosen](/images/changelog/go-live-picker.jpg)

## Donate button

We added a **Donate** button to the footer. Everything Immersive is brought to you by the Immersive Experience Institute, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and the button opens a donation form for the institute. The footer and the home page also show the institute's nonprofit status now.

![The site footer with the nonprofit line under the institute's logo and the Donate button](/images/changelog/donate-footer.jpg)
