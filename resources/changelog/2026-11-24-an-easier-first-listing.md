## Tips on every step

Listing an event asks a lot of questions, and some of them are easier to answer with a little context. What counts as a "run"? Should I pick a venue or somewhere else? Which advisories apply to my show?

Each step now has a **Tips** button at the top. Click it and you get a short explanation of that step, written by our team, with examples. Close it and you are right back where you were. The tips stay out of the way until you want them, so the questions themselves stay easy to read.

We also reworded parts of the listing flow to be clearer. For example, "Describe your event" is now "Describe the experience", and "Are there specific dates?" is now "When does it happen?"

## A friendlier first listing

If it is your first time listing with us, you now get a short welcome before you start. It explains that you make your organization first and then your listing, and it points out the Tips and Questions buttons in case you get stuck.

Once your organization is made, an empty events page used to leave you guessing what to do next. Now it shows one big **Create a new listing** button.

## More ways to link your organization

Organizations can now add their **Threads**, **Bluesky** and **LinkedIn** alongside Instagram, Twitter, Facebook and Patreon. They show up on your organization's page with the others.

## Smaller changes

- **Go Live dates.** You can no longer set a Go Live date after an event's last show, which would have kept the listing hidden until it was already over. The calendar stops at the last show, and you'll see a clear message if your dates change and the Go Live date ends up too late.
- **Telling shows with the same name apart.** When you name your listing and we find others with the same name, each match now says where it plays, so you can pick the right one.
- **Show pages** with two or three places now read naturally, like "New York and Brooklyn".
- **Maps** no longer crash on some phones when you leave the search page while the map is still zooming.
